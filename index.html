Ski pass

St. Anton is part of the Ski Arlberg region — one connected lift pass covers St. Anton, St. Christoph, Stuben, Lech, Zürs, and Warth-Schröcken (88 lifts, ~305km of pistes).

  • Indicative pricing (2025/26 season, adult): single day around €81.50 in high season; a 6-day pass was €380 in shoulder season — 2026/27 prices aren't published yet.
  • Where to buy: online in advance at skiarlberg.at (usually cheapest, skip the queue) or at any lift station ticket counter in St. Anton
  • Note: a €5 deposit applies for the reusable Ski Arlberg chip card

Equipment rental

Plenty of rental shops in St. Anton — Intersport Arlberg is the biggest local operator.

  • Indicative pricing: roughly €17–31+ per day depending on ski/snowboard tier (Economy / Premium / Superior) — multi-day rentals are cheaper per day (e.g. pay for 6 days, keep the gear for 7)
  • Where to book: intersport-arlberg.com — book online ahead of time to lock in your size and get a better rate; gear can usually be picked up from 4pm the day before you arrive
  • Alternative: Sport 2000 Rent

Zurich Airport → St. Anton am Arlberg

  • Journey: no direct train — expect one change (e.g. around Buchs SG / Feldkirch / Langen am Arlberg), total journey time roughly 2h45m–3h
  • Operators: SBB (Switzerland) and ÖBB (Austria)
  • Booking: plan is to book via the ÖBB website — advance fares can start from around €20 one-way
